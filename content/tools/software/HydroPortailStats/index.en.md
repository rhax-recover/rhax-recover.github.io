[HydroPortail](https://hydro.eaufrance.fr/) is the reference website for accessing hydrometric and hydrologic data in France. In addition to being a data portal, HydroPortail also provides statistical tools to analyze the series it stores, and in particular to estimate design discharge values (e.g. 10-year flood or 5-year drought). The R package [HydroPortailStats](https://github.com/benRenard/HydroPortailStats) contains all statistical computations made by HydroPortail, and even a few more such as the Bayesian estimation of a distribution or the analysis of incomplete and uncertain historical flood data.

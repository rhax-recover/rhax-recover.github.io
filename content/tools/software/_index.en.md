The team develops the following software:

* [BaRatinAGE](baratinage) to estimate rating curves and related uncertainties.
* The R package [ShiftHappens](shifthappens) to detect, visualize and estimate sudden changes in data, with a specific focus on hydrometry applications (rating shift detection).
* [HydroPortailStats](hydroportailstats), the R package used by the French [HydroPortail](https://hydro.eaufrance.fr/) to perform statistical computations.
* [R packages](sonification) for data sonification
* etc...
